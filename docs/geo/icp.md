# Roleflow ICP (for Meta ads)
Source: roleflow.io homepage (single-page site; no separate pricing/about/customers/use-case pages found). [I] = inference.

## Product
Done-for-you Instagram DM system: answers every lead in seconds, sends AI voice notes in the owner's cloned voice, auto follow-ups, books qualified leads on calendar. Roleflow builds and runs it. Pricing: $0 setup, $0 retainer, 5–7% of revenue it generates (rate set on demo call). Instagram DMs only.

## Qualification (stated on site)
- >20 DMs/day
- Offer >$1k
- Close rate >20%
- >$30k/month cash collected

## Target company
- Personal-brand, Instagram-driven businesses selling high-ticket ($1k+) offers. Site example: coaching.
- [I] Coaches (business, fitness, mindset, dating), course/mentorship creators, consultants, agency owners, info-product sellers, high-ticket service providers (med spa, aesthetics, real estate) with IG inbound.
- Size [I]: solo operator to ~20-person team, $30k–$500k+/mo revenue.

## Personas [I]
1. Founder/creator-coach (face of brand, does or oversees DMs) — primary buyer.
2. Owner who manages appointment setters/DM setters ("you chase setters instead of selling").
3. Ops/sales manager in a coaching company (influencer, not usually signer).
Titles/self-labels: Founder, CEO, Coach, Business Coach, Online Coach, Mentor, Consultant, Creator, Agency Owner.

## Pain points (from site)
- Leads wait hours; hot leads go cold / go to competitors
- Generic text replies; forgotten follow-ups
- Managing setters instead of selling
- Lost revenue (calculator: "$50K/mo")

## Value props
- Replies in seconds, 24/7
- Personal voice notes in your own voice
- Auto follow-ups + booked calls
- $0 upfront, pay only on results (5–7%)
- Fully built and run for you

## Likely objections [I unless noted]
- "Will it sound fake / like a bot?" (site addresses: real voice clone)
- Brand/authenticity risk, Instagram account safety/ToS
- Giving up % of revenue vs flat fee; how attribution is measured
- "Do I qualify?" (strict thresholds; quiz gate)
- Already have setters

## Meta targeting suggestions [I]
- Locations: US first; then CA, UK, AU, NZ, IE; optionally UAE (high-ticket coaching hub). English.
- Ages: 25–50 (core 28–45). All genders.
- Placements: Instagram Feed/Stories/Reels priority (audience lives on IG); FB feed secondary.
- Interests: Online coaching, Business coach, Life coaching, High ticket sales, Sales funnel, Entrepreneurship, Small business owners, Personal branding, Info products/online courses, Kajabi, Skool, ClickFunnels, GoHighLevel, ManyChat, Calendly, Alex Hormozi, Russell Brunson, Grant Cardone, Tony Robbins, Dan Henry, Iman Gadzhi, Instagram for Business, Influencer marketing.
- Behaviors: Business page admins (Facebook Page admins), Small business owners, Engaged shoppers (optional), Frequent international travelers (high-income proxy).
- Job-title-adjacent: Founder/CEO, Entrepreneur, Self-employed, Coach, Consultant (Meta employer/job targeting is limited; lean on interests + Advantage+ audience).
- Exclusions: students, job seekers ("remote jobs", "appointment setter jobs" interest), existing customers.
- Lookalikes: 1–3% LAL of (a) demo bookers, (b) qualified quiz completers, (c) closed clients weighted by revenue; IG engagers / video viewers (50%+) retargeting; website visitors who used the calculator.
- Note: Meta Special Ad Category not required; avoid personal-attribute phrasing ("Are you a coach losing money?") per Meta policy.
