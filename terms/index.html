Commercial clause

Roleflow charges no setup fee and no monthly retainer. The fee is 10% of the business growth the customer records on deals this system sourced. The customer enters that cash in their workspace. Roleflow’s share is 10% of the recorded amount. If the customer records nothing, nothing is owed for that work. The share is locked at 10% in the product. It is not a 10% range and it is not set again on a demo call.

The customer connects their own professional Instagram account and remains responsible for that account, including Meta’s policies. Roleflow does not ask for the Instagram password. Voice notes are recordings the customer uploads. Booking uses the customer’s Google Calendar when it is connected, and otherwise the public booking link published on roleflow.io.

If a lead asks whether they are talking to a bot or an AI, the assistant answers that Diego’s team uses an AI assistant to keep up with conversations, and that Diego personally reviews them and takes the calls.
