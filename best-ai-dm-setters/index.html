A human setter

A person is the baseline. They can take phone calls and read odd situations. They work a shift, and you manage them. We do not publish salary figures. Wrong for: a team that needs every Instagram DM answered in seconds, all day, without a setter to supervise. Roleflow vs a human setter.

Pricing models

Per message and per conversation bills grow when a post works. A flat retainer is owed in a quiet month too. A percent of revenue stays at zero until revenue shows up, and it only fits when the offer can pay for the work. Roleflow uses the percent model on Instagram. The other published prices above are monthly software plans.

How to choose

If you have 20+ Instagram DMs a day, an offer above $1,000, a close rate above 20%, and $30k+ collected, and you want the setter built and run with voice notes in your cloned voice, start with Roleflow. If you want to operate the software and pay a monthly plan across more channels, read the self-serve rows and confirm the [CONFIRM] cells on the vendor's site. If the sale happens on the phone and the DM is only a prelude a person must judge, keep a human setter in the process. Your closers still take Roleflow's booked calls.
