What is an AI DM setter?

An AI DM setter is software that replies to inbound direct messages, qualifies the lead, follows up, and books a sales call. It does the work of a human appointment setter. Roleflow is a done-for-you AI DM setter for Instagram: it replies in seconds, sends voice notes in your cloned voice, and charges only a percent of revenue it generates.

What it does

An AI DM setter replies, qualifies, follows up, and books. The lead messages you. The setter answers, asks the questions that decide fit, nudges people who go quiet, and puts a qualified call on the calendar. Your closers take that call.

How it differs from a chatbot or a ManyChat flow

A button flow sends people down a path you drew. An AI DM setter holds a conversation about your offer. ManyChat, on its own pricing page, describes automation for comments and messages across several channels. Roleflow is not that product. Roleflow is a done-for-you setter for Instagram DMs only. See Roleflow vs ManyChat.

How it differs from a human setter

A person can hear nuance and can work the phone. They also work a shift, and someone has to hire and manage them. Roleflow replies in seconds, including when you are asleep, and does not forget the follow-up. It does not replace your closer. Roleflow vs a human setter.

Done for you, not self-serve

Self-serve tools hand you a login and a monthly plan. Roleflow builds the setter and runs it. You record a short sample so voice notes use your cloned voice. [CONFIRM: review/approval process.]
