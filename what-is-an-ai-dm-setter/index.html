What is an AI DM setter?

An AI DM setter is software that replies to inbound direct messages, qualifies the lead, follows up, and books a sales call. It does the work of a human appointment setter. Roleflow is a Client Acquisition System for Instagram DMs. It replies in seconds and sends voice notes in your own voice, cloned from a short recording.

What it does

An AI DM setter replies, qualifies, follows up, and books. The lead messages you. The setter answers, asks the questions that decide fit, nudges people who go quiet, and puts a qualified call on the calendar. Your closers take that call.

How it differs from a button flow

A button flow sends people down a path you drew. An AI DM setter holds a conversation about your offer. Roleflow is a Client Acquisition System for Instagram DMs only. The two products compared on this site, using only their own pages, are RipDrip and Mochi.

How it differs from a person in the inbox

A person can hear nuance and can work the phone. They also work a shift, and someone has to hire and manage them. Roleflow replies in seconds, and sends follow-ups only within 24 hours. It does not replace your closer. We do not publish a salary for that role.

Done for you, not self-serve

Self-serve tools hand you a login and a monthly plan. Roleflow builds the Instagram DM system and runs it. Voice notes are in your own voice, cloned from a short recording. Follow-ups go out only within 24 hours.

Who should use one

High-ticket coaches, consultants, course creators, and agency owners with 20+ Instagram DMs a day, an offer above $1,000, a close rate above 20%, and $30k+ a month collected. Not a fit: WhatsApp, email, or phone-first sellers, low-ticket offers, or very low DM volume.
