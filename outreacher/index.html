Outreacher

Each business gets its own outreacher. After you pay, the Roleflow panel shows setup, then a browser where you sign in to Instagram. Roleflow does not see or store the password.

$249 setup + 10% of growth, 3-month minimum. If you cancel before the minimum ends, the outreacher continues until that date.

What you do

Create an account, complete checkout, and open Outreacher in the panel. When it is ready, enter the Instagram username and sign in inside the Roleflow browser. Later, Access opens that same browser. Sending stays off until you turn it on, and Pause stops it immediately.

Instagram can limit or close an account that sends messages this way. You accept that risk in the panel before checkout.
