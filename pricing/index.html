AI DM setter pricing

Roleflow charges $0 to set up and $0 as a monthly retainer. The fee is 5–7% of the revenue the Instagram DM system generates, and the exact rate is set on the demo call. You do not pay a flat software bill for the setter. Instagram DMs are the only channel.

Updated: 13 October 2026

The model

There is no setup invoice and no monthly retainer. Roleflow is paid 5–7% of the revenue the system generates. The exact rate is set on the demo call. [CONFIRM whether 5–7% is the final published range.] If the system generates nothing, you owe nothing for that work.

How attribution works

[CONFIRM: attribution method, e.g. booked calls sourced from Roleflow conversations that close.]

Illustrative formula, not a client result and not a promise: attributed revenue in a month × the rate from the demo call = the Roleflow share. If the attributed amount were $10,000 and the rate were 6%, the share would be $600. Your rate can sit anywhere from 5% to 7%. This is arithmetic, not a case study.

Who qualifies

20+ DMs a day, an offer above $1,000, a close rate above 20%, and $30k+ a month in cash collected. We fund the setup, so the offer has to be able to produce revenue from Instagram DMs.

Percent of revenue, or a flat fee

A flat monthly tool bill is predictable and you pay it even in a quiet month. A percent of revenue stays at $0 until the system generates revenue, and it moves with that revenue. The percent model fits a high-ticket Instagram offer with enough inbound. A flat fee can fit a team that wants software it operates itself. We do not publish a human setter's salary. The comparison with a person is about speed, coverage, follow-up, and management, on Roleflow vs a human setter.
