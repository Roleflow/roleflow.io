FAQ

What is an AI DM setter?

An AI DM setter is the name people search for software that answers inbound DMs, qualifies leads, follows up, and books calls. Roleflow is a Client Acquisition System. We build and run your Instagram DM system.

What does Roleflow do?

We build and run your Instagram DM system. Every lead is answered in seconds. Voice notes go out in your own voice, cloned from a short recording. Follow-ups are sent automatically, only within 24 hours. Qualified leads are booked on your calendar.

Who qualifies?

20+ DMs/day, an offer above $1k, a close rate above 20%, and $30k+/month cash collected.

Will it sound fake?

Voice notes are in your own voice, cloned from a short recording, and replies follow your offer and tone.

Do I still need my setters?

Roleflow handles first response, follow-ups within 24 hours, and booking. Your closers take the calls.

Which channels?

Instagram DMs only.
