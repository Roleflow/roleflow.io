Outbound

Outbound Manager

Inbound

Inbound Manager

FAQ

Straight answers.

What does Roleflow do?

Roleflow is an AI Client Acquisition Engine. We build and run your Instagram DM system. Every lead is answered in seconds. Voice notes go out in your own voice, cloned from a short recording. Follow-ups are sent automatically, only within 24 hours. Qualified leads are booked on your calendar. You watch that work in your panel.

Are the voice notes mine?

Yes. Voice notes are in your own voice, cloned from a short recording.

What do I see in the panel?

The outbound office, the inbound office, the CRM stages Cold, Warm, Hot, Converted, and Dead, plus Stats and Performance for the dates you choose. Activity on a desk comes from that business. Empty seats stay empty.

Who is it for?

High-ticket coaches, consultants, and agencies. You qualify with over 20 DMs a day, an offer above $1k, a closing rate above 20%, and over $30k a month in cash collected. The questions on this page apply that check.

What does it cost?

$0 upfront + 10% of revenue generated, no retainer

How do I start?

Answer the questions. If you qualify, the result shows the calendar link and a link to create your account. If you don't, retake the questions once your offer, price, close rate, or cash collected changes.
