How it works

Write it. Practice it. Turn it on.

01

Write the setter prompt

The AI DM setter uses the system prompt saved in the panel. You edit it there, and each save keeps a version.

02

Add your voice notes and calendar

Record the notes in the panel. Name one in the prompt and the setter sends that file on a normal DM. The booking link in the panel is what gets sent to book the call.

03

Practice the full conversation

The practice chat runs the thread on your prompt before anything is live. Practice never sends a message to Instagram.

04

Turn on only the agents you want

Lead finding, warm-up, the setter, follow-up, and comment voice each have an on/off switch. An agent with no saved switch stays off.

05

Reply when someone comments

Comment voice reads the comment. If it matches a keyword or an intent you wrote, it queues one private text reply. No ManyChat needed. The voice note mapped to that rule is logged and is not attached.

In the panel

The same controls you open when you sign in.

AI DM setter

Replies in Instagram DMs from the prompt the business writes. It stays off until a prompt is saved, the practice chat has been used, and the switch is turned on.

Recorded voice notes

Notes are recordings stored for that business. The setter sends the named recording on a normal DM.

Booking link

Each business saves its own calendar link. The setter inserts that link when the prompt asks for it.

Comment voice

Understands the comment against the keywords and intent you save, in English or Spanish. One private text reply. No ManyChat needed.

Practice chat

A full conversation in the panel, on the draft or saved prompt, before the setter goes live. Nothing from practice is sent.

Lead finding and warm-up

Separate agents, each with its own switch. They stay off until you turn them on. Roleflow does not run that scan from the page.

Follow-up

Sends only when the saved rules match, including a 24-hour window, and only while its switch is on.

English and Spanish

The panel language is English or Spanish. This site is available in both.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What does Roleflow do?

Roleflow is a Client Acquisition System for coaches and info businesses. The AI DM setter runs from the prompt you write and edit in the panel. It can send your recorded voice notes and your booking link. Comment voice reads a comment and can send one private text reply. No ManyChat needed.

Are the voice notes recordings?

Yes. You record them in the panel. When the prompt names a note, that file is sent on a normal DM. A comment reply is text. The note mapped to a comment is stored on the decision and is not attached.

Can I test a conversation before it goes live?

Yes. The practice chat runs the full thread on the saved or draft prompt. Practice never sends a message to Instagram. The setter stays off until you turn it on.

What do the switches control?

Lead finding, warm-up, the setter, follow-up, and comment voice each have an on/off switch. An agent with no saved switch stays off. Lead finding and warm-up do not run a scan from this page.

How do I get in?

Book a demo on the calendar. Access opens the account flow. If you are already signed in, that flow continues to your dashboard.
