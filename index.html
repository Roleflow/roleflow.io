FAQ

Straight answers.

What does Roleflow do?

It runs an AI acquisition team inside your own panel. Outbound Manager finds ideal prospects, warms them up, and reaches out. Inbound Manager qualifies direct messages, comments, and story replies, then books calls. Chief of Staff writes a daily summary. CRM, Stats, and Performance show the pipeline and the 10% share.

What do I pay?

Nothing to start, and nothing each month as a retainer. You pay 10% of the growth you record on deals this system sourced. Record nothing, and nothing is owed for that work.

Are the voice notes mine?

Yes. You record them and upload them. Personalised Outreach sends those recordings on the opener.

What do I see in the panel?

The outbound office, the inbound office, the CRM stages Cold, Warm, Hot, Converted, and Dead, plus Stats and Performance for the dates you choose. Activity on a desk comes from that business. Empty seats stay empty.

Who is it for?

High-ticket coaches, consultants, and agencies. To book, you need over 20 DMs a day, an offer above $1k, a closing rate above 20%, and over $30k a month in cash collected. The questions on this page apply that check.

How do I start?

Answer the questions. If you qualify, the result shows the booking link and a link to create your account. If you don't, retake the questions once your offer, price, close rate, or cash collected changes.
