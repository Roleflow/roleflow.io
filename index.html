How a lead moves

From a comment to a booking link.

A live sketch of the path. Scroll the steps, or let it play. A comment reply is text. The voice note is a recording on the DM.

  1. 01

    A comment arrives

    Someone writes on a post. Voice Gen has a Comment recording, and a note telling the AI when to send it, in English or Spanish.

  2. 02

    It reads the comment

    If the comment matches, one private text reply is queued. No separate keyword tool. The setter itself stays off until you turn it on.

  3. 03

    A voice note on the DM

    On a normal DM, the setter sends the recording the prompt names. A note mapped to a comment is logged and is not attached.

  4. 04

    Your booking link goes out

    When the prompt asks for it, the setter sends the calendar link saved for that business. The reply waits a random time between the minimum and maximum you set.

Services

What Roleflow does.

AI DM setter

Replies in Instagram DMs from the prompt the business writes. It stays off until a prompt is saved, the practice chat has been used, and the switch is turned on.

Recorded voice notes

Notes are recordings stored for that business. The setter sends the named recording on a normal DM.

Booking link

Each business saves its own calendar link. The setter inserts that link when the prompt asks for it.

Practice chat

A full conversation in the panel, on the draft or saved prompt, before the setter goes live. Nothing from practice is sent.

Reply delay

You set a minimum and a maximum in seconds. The live reply waits a random time between them. Zero is allowed. Practice can skip the wait.

Follow-up

Sends only when the saved rules match, including a 24-hour window, and only while its switch is on.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What does Roleflow do?

Roleflow is an AI Client Acquisition Engine for coaches and info businesses. The AI DM setter runs from the prompt you write and edit in the panel. It can send your recorded voice notes and your booking link. Voice Gen includes a Comment recording, and a note telling the AI when to send it.

How does Roleflow get paid?

Roleflow is 100% revenue share. There is no setup fee and no monthly retainer. The fee is 10% of the business growth you record on deals Roleflow sourced. If you record nothing, nothing is owed. That share is the fee in the terms. It is not a promise of income. Results vary; not a guarantee.

Are the voice notes recordings?

Yes. You record them in the panel. When the prompt names a note, that file is sent on a normal DM. A comment reply is text. The note mapped to a comment is stored on the decision and is not attached.

Can I test a conversation before it goes live?

Yes. The practice chat runs the full thread on the saved or draft prompt. Practice never sends a message to Instagram. The setter stays off until you turn it on.

Does the setter reply the instant a message arrives?

You choose a minimum and a maximum in seconds. The live reply waits a random time between them. Zero is allowed. Practice can skip the wait.

What do the switches control?

Lead finding, warm-up, the setter, and follow-up each have an on/off switch. An agent with no saved switch stays off. Lead finding and warm-up do not run a scan from this page.

How do I get in?
