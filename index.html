Questions

What does Roleflow install?

Roleflow installs a client acquisition system on your Instagram and runs it with several AI agents working together. DM setting is one part: it answers Instagram DMs, qualifies the lead, and books a call, including voice notes in a clone of your voice. Follow-ups send another note on quiet threads, inside 24 hours. Outreach starts new conversations. Comment targeting comments on posts you choose. A live comment source is not connected yet, so that agent does not comment on its own today. Stats tracking reads totals from every station.

Is it really my voice?

Yes. We clone your voice from a short recording, so the voice notes leads receive sound like you.

What is the fee?

There is no setup fee. Roleflow charges a 10% fee. There is no monthly retainer.

Why can't everyone book a demo?

We fund the setup, so we only work with businesses where the system can realistically generate revenue. To qualify you need over 20 DMs a day, an offer above $1k, a closing rate above 20%, and over $30k a month in cash collected.

Which account does it run on?

Your Instagram account. Roleflow does not run this system on WhatsApp, email, or phone.

What do I still do?

You take the sales calls. Roleflow handles the first response, the follow-ups, and the booking.

What if I don't qualify now?

Retake the quiz once your offer, price point, or inbound volume changes.
