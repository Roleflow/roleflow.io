Questions

What does Roleflow install?

Roleflow installs an AI agent system on your Instagram account and runs it. The stations are DM setting, follow-ups, outreach, and target comments. DM setting answers leads, qualifies them, and books calls, including voice notes in a clone of your voice. Follow-ups send another note on quiet threads, inside 24 hours. Outreach starts new conversations. Target comments is the station for commenting on posts you choose. A live comment source is not connected yet, so that station does not comment on its own today.

Is it really my voice?

Yes. We clone your voice from a short recording, so the voice notes leads receive sound like you.

What does the 5 to 7% mean?

We take 5 to 7% of the revenue the system generates. There is no monthly retainer. If the system generates nothing, you owe nothing. Your exact rate is confirmed on the demo call. A booked call is not a sale. The fee is on revenue.

Why can't everyone book a demo?

We fund the setup, so we only work with businesses where the system can realistically generate revenue. To qualify you need over 20 DMs a day, an offer above $1k, a closing rate above 20%, and over $30k a month in cash collected.

Which account does it run on?

Your Instagram account. Roleflow does not run this system on WhatsApp, email, or phone.

What do I still do?

You take the sales calls. Roleflow handles the first response, the follow-ups, and the booking.

What if I don't qualify now?

Retake the quiz once your offer, price point, or inbound volume changes.
