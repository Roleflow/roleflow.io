Agency owners who sell a high-ticket service from their own Instagram still lose leads in the DMs. Roleflow replies in seconds, sends voice notes in your cloned voice, follows up, and books the call. Your clients' inboxes are not this product

Agency owners get pitched tools that promise to run every client's Instagram. That is not Roleflow. Roleflow runs the agency owner's own Instagram DMs: the ones where a founder asks if you can take on their account, what a retainer costs, and whether you have room this month. If you sell a high-ticket service from your personal brand, you have the same leak as a coach. Leads wait. Follow-ups slip because you are in a client delivery. Setters on your team get managed instead of you selling the next account.

The conversation is commercial. Budget, scope, and timing. Roleflow replies in seconds, sends voice notes in your cloned voice, follows up, and books a qualified call. It does not log into a client's profile. It does not post. It does not replace the operators who deliver the work you sell.

Qualification, stated plainly

20+ DMs a day on the Instagram account we would run, an offer above $1,000, a close rate above 20%, and $30k+ a month collected. An agency that sells $500 setup fees, or that lives in email and Upwork, does not qualify. Instagram DMs only. $0 setup, $0 retainer, 5–7% of the revenue this system generates.
