Consultants sell through conversations, and Instagram DMs are often the first one. Roleflow is an AI Client Acquisition Engine that replies in seconds, sends voice notes in your own voice, cloned from a short recording, and books a qualified call. We build it and we run it.

The DM is the first meeting

Consultants do not sell a seat in a cohort. They sell a diagnosis. The Instagram DM is often a founder describing a messy problem in four lines and asking if you can help. The wrong reply is a PDF. The right reply asks what the engagement would have to change, whether the budget matches a project above $1,000, and whether a call is worth both calendars. Roleflow runs that first conversation. You still do the actual consulting.

The pain is familiar and a bit more expensive than a coach's missed DM, because one project can be the month. Leads wait. Follow-ups die. You either answer at midnight or you pay a setter who does not know your practice well enough to avoid a bad-fit call. Roleflow qualifies before the call. It follows up within 24 hours if the thread goes quiet. Voice notes in your own voice, cloned from a short recording, matter here because a consultant's trust is the voice, not a branded chatbot.

Scope

Instagram DMs only. If your pipeline is referrals and the phone, this is the wrong product. If your pipeline is inbound DMs from posts and you close above 20% on calls, with 20+ DMs a day and $30k+ a month collected, it is the right one. Follow-ups go out only within 24 hours.

Consultants also get DMs that are not buyers: students, vendors, and people who want a free audit. The setter's job is to tell those apart before a 45-minute call is spent on someone who cannot hire you. Roleflow uses your questions for that filter. It does not invent a case study or a win rate. If a fact is not confirmed, it is left out instead of being dressed up. The same rule keeps salary figures and star ratings off this page. We leave both of them out.
