AI DM setter for consultants

Consultants sell through conversations, and Instagram DMs are often the first one. Roleflow is a done-for-you AI DM setter that replies in seconds, sends personal voice notes you record, and books a qualified call. $0 setup, no retainer, 10% of revenue it generates. Phone-first firms are outside the fit.

The DM is the first meeting

Consultants do not sell a seat in a cohort. They sell a diagnosis. The Instagram DM is often a founder describing a messy problem in four lines and asking if you can help. The wrong reply is a PDF. The right reply asks what the engagement would have to change, whether the budget matches a project above $1,000, and whether a call is worth both calendars. Roleflow runs that first conversation. You still do the actual consulting.

The pain is familiar and a bit more expensive than a coach's missed DM, because one project can be the month. Leads wait. Follow-ups die. You either answer at midnight or you pay a setter who does not know your practice well enough to avoid a bad-fit call. Roleflow qualifies before the call. It follows up when the founder goes into a board week and disappears. Personal voice notes you record matter here because a consultant's trust is the voice, not a branded chatbot.

Scope

Instagram DMs only. If your pipeline is referrals and the phone, this is the wrong product.
