AI DM setter for coaches

An AI DM setter for coaches replies to Instagram leads about your program, qualifies them, and books the sales call. Roleflow does that work as a Client Acquisition System, with voice notes in your own voice, cloned from a short recording. We build it and we run it.

The inbox after you post

A coach posts a client story and the DMs arrive while you are on a call. Someone asks if you still take one-to-one clients. Someone else asks for the price. A third person says they will "circle back." By the time you answer, the first two are already talking to another coach. That delay is the whole problem Roleflow is built for. It is not a content tool and it does not post for you. It answers the Instagram conversation that the content started.

The pains are the ones coaches already name. Leads wait hours. The reply that finally goes out is a generic text. The follow-up never happens because the thread fell under newer messages. And if you hired a setter, you now manage that person instead of coaching. Roleflow takes the first response, the follow-up, and the booking. You keep the sessions and the sales calls.

How it works for a coaching offer

You send a short recording. Voice notes go out in your own voice, cloned from that recording, and Roleflow writes replies that follow your offer and your tone. A lead who asks about a high-ticket program gets a conversation, not a link dump: what they want, whether they can invest, and whether a call makes sense. If they qualify, the call lands on your calendar. If they go quiet, the follow-up still goes out within 24 hours. Voice notes in your own voice, cloned from a short recording go out so the thread sounds like you. Follow-ups go out only within 24 hours.

This is done for you. You do not build a button flow and you do not staff a night shift. Roleflow is a Client Acquisition System. We build and run your Instagram DM system, with voice notes in your own voice, cloned from a short recording.

Who qualifies

Coaches with 20+ DMs a day, an offer above $1,000, a close rate above 20%, and $30k+ a month collected.
