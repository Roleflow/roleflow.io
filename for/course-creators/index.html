Launches make a queue, not a relationship

A course creator can have a quiet week and then a post or a cart open that drops a hundred Instagram DMs in an afternoon. The questions repeat: is this cohort still open, does it fit someone at my level, what is the price, can I pay later. Answering them by hand means the launch thread and the DM thread fight for the same hour. A generic "link in bio" reply wastes the people who were ready to talk. Roleflow answers each DM in seconds, qualifies against your offer, follows up only within 24 hours, and books calls for the high-ticket version of what you sell.

Low-ticket self-study courses are the wrong offer. Roleflow is for an offer above $1,000, where a call is how you sell. Voice notes in your own voice, cloned from a short recording, matter on a launch because the buyer followed you for the way you talk. A template does not sound like the person in the reels. Follow-ups go out only within 24 hours.

After the spike

The dangerous day is not launch day. It is three days later, when the unanswered "thinking about it" threads are buried. Follow-ups go out only within 24 hours. Booking is the job. You still teach and you still take sales calls. We build and run the Instagram DM system.
