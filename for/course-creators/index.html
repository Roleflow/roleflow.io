AI DM setter for course creators

Course creators get a spike of Instagram DMs when a post or launch goes out. Roleflow answers each one in seconds, qualifies, follows up, and books calls for a high-ticket offer, with personal voice notes you record. It is done for you. We build the system and we run it.

Launches make a queue, not a relationship

A course creator can have a quiet week and then a post or a cart open that drops a hundred Instagram DMs in an afternoon. The questions repeat: is this cohort still open, does it fit someone at my level, what is the price, can I pay later. Answering them by hand means the launch thread and the DM thread fight for the same hour. A generic "link in bio" reply wastes the people who were ready to talk. Roleflow answers each DM in seconds, qualifies against your offer, follows up after the launch noise, and books calls for the high-ticket version of what you sell.

Low-ticket self-study courses are the wrong offer. Roleflow is for an offer above $1,000, where a call is how you sell. Personal voice notes you record matter on a launch because the buyer followed you for the way you talk. A template does not sound like the person in the reels. [CONFIRM: review/approval process.]

After the spike

The dangerous day is not launch day. It is three days later, when the unanswered "thinking about it" threads are buried. Automatic follow-up is the job. Booking is the job. You still teach and you still take sales calls. We build and run the setter.
