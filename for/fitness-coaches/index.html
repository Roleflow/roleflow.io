AI DM setter for fitness coaches

Fitness coaches get DMs after reels asking if spots are open. Roleflow answers those Instagram messages in seconds, with personal voice notes you record, and books people who fit your offer. It is done for you. We build it and we run it. It fits coaches above the qualification line, not low-ticket challenges.

Reels fill the inbox. The program still has a price.

A fitness coach posts a training clip and the DMs ask three things: do you have spots, is this for beginners or for people who already train, and what does it cost. The offer Roleflow is for is the high-ticket one, above $1,000, not a $29 challenge. The lead who can pay is easy to lose because they messaged ten coaches from the same explore page.

Speed matters here more than a clever script. They are on their phone between sessions. A reply in seconds, including a personal voice note you recorded, is the difference between a conversation and a seen-zone. Follow-up matters because fitness leads say "after this trip" and mean it until nobody writes back. Roleflow sends that follow-up. It books the call when they fit. It does not write your program and it does not promise a body result. We do not publish client transformations.

Done for you, around a coaching week

You record your voice once. Roleflow runs the Instagram DMs while you are on the gym floor or in sessions. Qualification is the same bar as the rest of Roleflow: 20+ DMs a day, offer above $1,000, close rate above 20%, $30k+ a month collected. A coach selling only low-ticket app subscriptions does not qualify. WhatsApp check-ins with current clients are a different channel. Roleflow does not run those.
