AI DM setter for business coaches

Business coaches lose deals when a DM about a high-ticket program sits for hours. Roleflow is a done-for-you AI DM setter for Instagram that replies in seconds, sends voice notes in your cloned voice, and books qualified calls. You pay $0 to start and 5–7% of revenue it generates. It is not a WhatsApp tool.

Application calls, not content likes

A business coach sells a program that starts above $1,000. The Instagram DM is the application before the application. People ask about revenue, about whether you work with their niche, and about the time commitment. Those questions decide the call. If they sit unanswered until the evening, the buyer with money has already booked someone who replied at lunch.

The specific mess is the setter layer. Many business coaches already pay someone to "handle DMs." That person works a shift, writes in a voice that is almost yours, and still misses Friday night. You review their threads instead of selling. Roleflow replaces that first-response job. It does not replace the closer who runs the sales call, and it does not coach your clients.

What the conversation has to do

The reply has to sound like the person whose face is on the profile. Roleflow sends voice notes in your cloned voice and written replies that follow your offer. It asks the qualifying questions you already use: offer fit, ability to invest, and whether a call is the next step. Quiet threads get a follow-up. Qualified threads get a calendar booking. A normal week for this buyer is a mix of podcast clips, a live, and a DM from someone who already bought a cheaper product and now wants the real program. Roleflow does not upsell inside someone else's community and it does not email the list.
