AI DM setter for business coaches

Business coaches lose deals when a DM about a high-ticket program sits for hours. Roleflow is an AI Client Acquisition Engine for Instagram. It replies in seconds, sends voice notes in your own voice, cloned from a short recording, and books qualified calls. We build it and we run it.

Application calls, not content likes

A business coach sells a program that starts above $1,000. The Instagram DM is the application before the application. People ask about revenue, about whether you work with their niche, and about the time commitment. Those questions decide the call. If they sit unanswered until the evening, the buyer with money has already booked someone who replied at lunch.

The specific mess is the setter layer. Many business coaches already pay someone to "handle DMs." That person works a shift, writes in a voice that is almost yours, and still misses Friday night. You review their threads instead of selling. Roleflow replaces that first-response job. It does not replace the closer who runs the sales call, and it does not coach your clients.

What the conversation has to do

The reply has to sound like the person whose face is on the profile. Roleflow sends voice notes in your own voice, cloned from a short recording, and written replies that follow your offer. It asks the qualifying questions you already use: offer fit, ability to invest, and whether a call is the next step. Quiet threads get a follow-up within 24 hours. Qualified threads get a calendar booking. Follow-ups go out only within 24 hours.

Roleflow builds and runs the Instagram DM system. That is a different arrangement from hiring a setter and paying for a software subscription. We do not publish salary numbers. The point is the model: done for you, Instagram only.

Fit

20+ DMs a day, offer above $1,000, close rate above 20%, $30k+ a month collected. A business coach who sells a $300 workshop, or who closes on WhatsApp, is not the account we set up. Qualified leads are booked on your calendar. We build and run the Instagram DM system.

A normal week for this buyer is a mix of podcast clips, a live, and a DM from someone who already bought a cheaper product and now wants the real program. Roleflow does not upsell inside someone else's community and it does not email the list. It stays in Instagram DMs, asks whether the high-ticket program is the right next step, and books the call only when the answers match. That keeps the closer's calendar for people who can actually buy. Your closers still take every call that lands on the calendar.
