Roleflow vs a human setter

A human setter can read nuance and take phone calls, on a shift, and someone has to hire and manage them. Roleflow replies on Instagram in seconds, all day, sends voice notes in your cloned voice, follows up, and books calls. Setup is $0. The fee is 5–7% of revenue the system generates. Closers still take the sales calls.

What stays with a person

A human setter can sit on a phone call and can judge a strange situation. Roleflow does not take the sales call. Your closers do. Use a person when the work is the phone, the judgment, or a channel Roleflow does not run.

What Roleflow takes

The Instagram inbox: first response, follow-up, qualification, and the booked call. You are not managing a setter's hours for that slice of the work.

Choose them if

The sale needs a person on the phone, or the lead does not arrive as an Instagram DM.

Choose Roleflow if

The leak is slow Instagram replies, missed follow-ups, and time spent managing setters, and you meet the qualification line.
