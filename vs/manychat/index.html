Roleflow vs ManyChat

Roleflow is a done-for-you setter for Instagram DMs, with voice notes in your cloned voice and a 5–7% fee instead of a retainer. ManyChat is a self-serve product for building flows across channels, with published plan prices. Choose Roleflow if you do not want to build it. Choose ManyChat if you want a flow builder.
